Filler questions, answered

How long do dermal fillers last?

Most hyaluronic acid fillers last six to eighteen months depending on the product and area. The collagen-stimulating option can last around a year or more. Melissa will estimate longevity for your specific plan.

Are dermal fillers reversible?

Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase if needed. The collagen-stimulating option is not reversible, which Melissa will discuss with you.

Does filler look natural?

It does when placed conservatively by an experienced injector. Melissa uses the minimal product necessary and builds gradually so results look balanced, never overdone.

Is there downtime after filler?

Most patients resume normal activities the same day. Temporary swelling, tenderness or small bruises are common and usually settle within a few days to a week.

How much do dermal fillers cost?

Fillers are priced per syringe, generally $650 to $800 depending on the area. New clients save $99, and Cherry financing lets you pay over time.

Who is a good candidate?

Adults in good general health with realistic expectations who are bothered by thinning lips, flat cheeks, hollows under the eyes or deep smile lines and want a natural, refreshed look.
